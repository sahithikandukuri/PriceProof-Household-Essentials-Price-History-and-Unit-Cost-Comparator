🔒 Pricing Audit Integrity

PriceProof maintains an append-only audit trail. Historical price observations are never overwritten or deleted, ensuring authentic trend transparency for consumers.

  • Price updates immediately recalculate lowest unit cost badges across retailers.
  • If the updated price drops below any user's target price alert, in-app notifications are dispatched instantly.
  • All adjustments record the administrator ID and timestamp.
